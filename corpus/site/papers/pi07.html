pi0.7: a Steerable Generalist Robotic Foundation Model with Emergent Capabilities

Physical Intelligence · arXiv 2604.15483 2026

PDF arXiv 2604.15483 project none

Physical-Intelligence/openpi documents pi0, pi0-FAST, and pi0.5. It does not contain pi0.7. That repository was not cloned as this paper's code.

pi0.7 is a 5B flow-matching VLA, a Gemma3 4B backbone plus an 860M action expert, trained with a richer prompt than a task sentence: detailed language, episode metadata, control-modality labels, and multi-view subgoal images. A separate 14B BAGEL model generates those subgoals. The paper's claim is that this context lets the VLA train on mixed-quality and non-robot data without averaging incompatible strategies. There is no public implementation to link.

Steerable VLA plus a subgoal world model · Compositional skill recombination and failure recovery · Local note: scaled-down expert via cached VLM features. The paper's full model is about 5B, and the subgoal model is a separate 14B.

Model

Gemma3 4B VLM, including a 400M vision encoder, with a MEM-style video history encoder that emits a fixed token count for any history length. An 860M flow-matching action expert attends to the VLM. Knowledge insulation trains the VLM with FAST tokens and stops action-expert gradients from entering the VLM. The subgoal generator is a BAGEL 14B model, separate from the 5B policy.

Objectives

Steerable action likelihood verified

\max_\theta\ \mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{D}}\big[\log\pi_\theta(a_{t:t+H}\mid o_{t-T:t},\mathcal{C}_t)\big]

The written objective is a chunk log-likelihood conditioned on recent observations and the full context. The paper says the flow-matching expert optimizes a lower bound of that likelihood rather than the closed form. Context is what makes a failed episode usable: the metadata tells the model how the episode was performed.

  • \mathcal{C}_t — Language, episode metadata, control modality, and subgoal images.
  • H — Action-chunk horizon. A shorter prefix is executed.
pdf: Eq. 1

Subgoal flow matching verified

\max_\psi\ \mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{D}_g}\big[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CFM}}(g^\star_t,\ g_\psi(o_t,\hat{\ell}_t,m))\big]

The world model is trained with conditional flow matching to the image at the end of a labeled segment. At runtime it fills in the subgoal images the VLA was trained to consume.

  • g^\star_t — Observed frame at the end of the segment.
  • m — Episode metadata.
