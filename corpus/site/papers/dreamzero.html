World Action Models are Zero-shot Policies

Seonghyeon Ye et al. (NVIDIA) · arXiv 2602.15922 2026

PDF arXiv 2602.15922 project repo ab790c19 official

Official inference and training code, shallow-cloned at this commit. Checkpoints were not downloaded.

DreamZero fine-tunes a pretrained video diffusion backbone to denoise future video latents and robot actions together. The released training path is a causal Wan model: one DiT sees video tokens and action registers, and the loss is a weighted MSE on both velocity targets. The paper's main model is Wan2.1-I2V-14B. The class defaults opened in CausalWanModel are a smaller shape, so those defaults are not the 14B width. Closed-loop inference replaces predicted frames with the real observation in the KV cache after each chunk.

Joint video-action diffusion, a 14B world-action model · Physical-motion generalization of VLAs that only imitate actions · Local note: not recommended, about 40–80+ GB. The paper's speed table uses H100 and GB200.

Task contract

What a new task has to supply before a slot from this method can move.

EmbodimentAgiBot G1 teleop and a Franka robot on DROID.
ActionRelative joint positions normalized to [-1, 1]. AgiBot uses 48 action steps at 30 Hz. DROID uses 24 action steps at 15 Hz.
HorizonBoth settings cover 1.6 seconds. Training packs M=4 chunks of K=2 latent frames. Closed-loop inference writes the real frame back into the KV cache.
ContactVideo, text, proprioception, and action. A wrench input was not recorded.
LatencyDreamZero-Flash is reported at about 7 Hz on GB200, 5.7 s down to 150 ms. The README also quotes about 0.6 s on GB200 and about 3 s on H100 with DiT caching.
DataAgiBot is about 500 hours and 7.2k episodes. DROID is a separate 100k-step run. Video is sampled at 5 fps.

Action registers inside the same DiT that denoises video latents.

Objective verified

One weighted MSE on the video velocity and the action velocity.

In: Paired video and action trajectories, split into chunks.

Out: A joint flow-matching loss.

Frozen: VAE, image encoder, and text encoder. Trained: DiT, state encoder, action encoder, and action decoder. 100k steps, global batch 128.

AgiBot G1 teleop paper verified recorded

About 500 hours, 7.2k episodes, 22 environments. Episodes average about 4.4 minutes and about 42 subtasks. Video is sampled at 5 fps and actions at 30 Hz, horizon 48, which is 1.6 seconds per chunk.

Idle actions are filtered. Relative joint positions are the default action. Training samples a trajectory and splits it into M=4 chunks of K=2 latent frames.

The data transform package includes crop, color jitter, and related video transforms. Which of those the 14B run enabled was not confirmed in the training config.

Model

A Wan video DiT with action and state registers. The VAE, image encoder, and text encoder stay frozen. The DiT, state encoder, action encoder, and action decoder train. CausalWanModel is the backbone class. Its __init__ defaults (dim 2048, 32 layers, 16 heads, ffn 8192) are not the Wan2.1-I2V-14B shape; the 14B width is selected by the checkpoint config.

14B for the Wan2.1-I2V-14B runs. A 5B Wan ablation is also reported. Added parameters are the state and action encoder/decoder. verified

Objectives

Shared interpolation verified

z_{t_k}^{k}=t_k z_1^{k}+(1-t_k)z_0^{k},\quad a_{t_k}^{k}=t_k a_1^{k}+(1-t_k)a_0^{k}

Clean latent and clean action sit at t=1. Noise sits at t=0. In the default objective a chunk shares one timestep across video and action. DreamZero-Flash draws them separately: video time from Beta(7, 1), action time uniform.

  • z_1 — Clean VAE latent of the video chunk.
  • a_1 — Normalized action chunk.
pdf: Eq. 2 and Algorithm 1 · WANPolicyHead.forward · groot/vla/model/dreamzero/action_head/wan_flow_matching_action_tf.py:603-810

Joint velocity loss verified

\mathcal{L}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}\frac{1}{K}\sum_k w(t_k)\big\lVert u_\theta([z_{t_k}^{k},a_{t_k}^{k}];\mathcal{C}_k,c,q_k,t_k)-v^{k}\big\rVert^2

The target velocity is the clean pair minus the noise pair. Context C_k is the clean previous chunks (teacher forcing). The opened implementation splits this into a weighted video MSE plus a weighted action MSE, and gates the action term with the action mask and has_real_action.

  • v^{k} — [z_1, a_1] - [z_0, a_0].
  • w(t) — Scheduler training weight.
  • \mathcal{C}_k — Clean latents and actions of earlier chunks.
