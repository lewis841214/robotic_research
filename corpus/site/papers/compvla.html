CompVLA: A Variable Compliance Vision-Language-Action Model for Contact-rich Manipulation

Jongmin Kim, Junsu Ha, Che-Sang Park, Minchang Song, Hyeokju Jeong, Himchan Hwang, Jianlong Fu, Frank C. Park · CoRL 2026 2026

PDF arXiv 2609.23614 project none

The project page and the paper describe a released demonstration dataset. No public training repository was found in this pass.

CompVLA adds a second flow-matching expert to a pi0-style VLA. The action expert predicts a TCP twist and gripper command. The compliance expert, conditioned on that trajectory, predicts a diagonal stiffness and a virtual displacement. A geometric impedance controller turns those into directional compliance and a pressing force. The paper learns stiffness Kd, not a proportional gain Kp. The local landscape row's Kp label is therefore the planning note, not the paper's output. No code was linked because no repository was released in the sources opened here.

Variable-compliance VLA. The local note says Kp and Kd plus actions. The paper's learned compliance outputs are diagonal stiffness and virtual displacement. · Rigid position control on contact-rich tasks · Local note: ideal stack-on, about 3–4 GB VRAM

Model

A pi0 backbone (PaliGemma VLM plus an action expert) gains a compliance expert initialized by copying the action expert. Both experts attend to the VLM with a blockwise causal mask. Weights start from lerobot/pi0_base. The compliance expert also sees a 13-d relative-pose condition packed into the noisy compliance input.

Objectives

Noisy target path verified

\tilde{Y}_t(\tau)=\tau\epsilon+(1-\tau)Y_t

Tau 1 is noise and tau 0 is the data. Because inference integrates from 1 to 0, the regression target is epsilon minus Y.

  • Y_t — Clean action sequence or clean compliance sequence.
  • \epsilon — Gaussian noise of the same shape.
  • \tau — Flow time, uniform on (0, 1).
pdf: Appendix A, eq. 9

Joint flow-matching loss verified

\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_a+\mathcal{L}_c,\quad \mathcal{L}_a=\mathbb{E}\lVert v^a_\theta(\tilde{Y}^a,\tau\mid O_t,l)-(\epsilon^a-Y^a)\rVert_2^2,\quad \mathcal{L}_c=\mathbb{E}\lVert v^c_\theta(\tilde{Y}^c,\tau\mid O_t,l,Z_t)-(\epsilon^c-Y^c)\rVert_2^2

The action expert is conditioned on the observation and language. The compliance expert is also conditioned on the relative TCP trajectory. During training that trajectory is the demonstration. During inference it is the action expert's prediction. Joint angles are an auxiliary training target and are dropped at inference.

  • Y^a — TCP twists and gripper commands over the chunk.
  • Y^c — Diagonal stiffness and virtual displacement over the chunk.
  • Z_t — Relative TCP poses and gripper commands, eq. 8.
pdf: Appendix A, eq. 10–12
